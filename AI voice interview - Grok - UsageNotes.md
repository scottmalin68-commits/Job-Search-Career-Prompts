## AI Voice Interview Utility - Quick Usage Notes
**Target App:** Grok / X App (Voice Mode)  
**Framework Version:** 1.1.1  
**Author:** Scott Malin, CISSP  

- Replace any placeholder details if you already know your target role, or let the interviewer prompt you during Phase 1 Setup.
- Speak or paste this whole prompt block when you open voice mode in the Grok/X app. It takes about 30 to 40 seconds to dictate or copy-paste.
- Once the interviewer confirms your details, it drops straight into the execution phase (asking up to 5 questions total).
- The strict state lock and persona rules keep the AI from breaking character or drifting off-topic during long sessions.
- Mid-session commands to try: "Make it tougher," "Be more skeptical," "Probe deeper on that," "Give feedback now," "Retry my last answer," or "End interview."
- After the 5th question or whenever you say "wrap up," the prompt forces an exit and generates your final scored report.

**Easiest Way to Run the Prompt on Mobile**
1. **One-time setup**: Save the prompt in a pinned note (Apple Notes, Google Keep) or email it to yourself. Title it "Grok Mock Prompt" for fast lookup.
2. **When ready**: Open X app, open Grok chat, tap the mic/waveform for voice mode, tap the text input field, paste the prompt, and send.
3. **If voice starts early**: Just say "Wait" or "Read this first," then paste the prompt. 
4. **Pro tip**: Set up iOS Text Replacement or an Android clipboard shortcut like `,grokmock` to drop it in instantly without opening your notes app.
