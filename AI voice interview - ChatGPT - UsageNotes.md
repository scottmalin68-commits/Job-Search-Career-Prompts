# Quick Usage Notes for ChatGPT Advanced Voice

## AI Voice Interview – v1.1.1
Author: Scott Malin, CISSP  
Version: 1.1.1  

- Activation: In the ChatGPT app (iOS/Android), tap the headphone/waveform icon in the chat to enable Advanced Voice[cite: 3, 4].
- Paste the v1.1.1 prompt into the chat (you can send it in text first and then switch to voice mode, or paste it directly while in voice mode)[cite: 3, 4].
- Setup Phase: The AI will verbally walk you through 5 setup questions covering job title/level/industry, interview type, focus areas, preferred tone, and seniority calibration[cite: 3].
- Interview Flow: Expect exactly 5 primary questions with built-in follow-up cycles for vague or shallow answers[cite: 3]. Mid-session commands you can use include "Make it harder," "Follow up on that," "Summarize my performance so far," or "End and give full report"[cite: 4].
- Final Report: After the fifth question or when you say "end interview," it delivers an out-of-character structured report with an overall score out of 10, category breakdowns, strengths, areas to improve, and 3–5 actionable tips[cite: 3].
- Post-Session: Follow the instructions provided at the end to share the conversation link or copy the transcript[cite: 3, 4].

**Easiest Way to Run on Mobile**
1. **One-time setup**: Save the full prompt in a pinned note or use a keyboard shortcut (like iOS Text Replacement or an Android clipboard manager) to assign a quick shortcut like `,chatmock` that expands instantly[cite: 4].
2. **When ready**: Open the ChatGPT app, paste the prompt into a new chat (send via text first if voice-only blocks pasting), then tap the headphone icon to start your Advanced Voice session[cite: 4].
