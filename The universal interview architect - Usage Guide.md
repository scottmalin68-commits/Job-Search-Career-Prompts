# USER GUIDE: The Universal Interview Architect
**Author:** Scott Malin, CISSP
**Version:** 4.0.0  
**Date:** 2026-09-09  

## Purpose
The Universal Interview Architect is a **Reverse Engineering Engine**. It destroys corporate fluff, identifies the specific anxieties of a hiring team, and builds an evidence-based strategy focused on measurable ROI. It maps your experience directly to the specific power dynamic of your interviewer while equipping you to detect cultural traps and systemic operational red flags before signing an offer.

## What the Engine Gathers
To get a High-Fidelity output, the Architect synthesizes six core data streams:
1. **Out-of-Bounds & Posting Red Flags:** Pinpoints candidate discussion risks alongside posting-level systemic traps (e.g., hidden 24/7 on-call burdens, scope creep).
2. **The Industry Pain Guess:** Locates the specific "fire" or operational bottleneck the company is desperate to solve.
3. **The Reality Gap:** Uncovers where the written JD differs from daily, chaotic execution.
4. **The Stakeholder Lens:** Decodes exact interviewer priorities across Recruiter, Hiring Manager, and Skip-Level perspectives.
5. **Red Flag Interrogation Questions:** Equips candidates with surgical, reverse-engineering questions paired with response-decoding guides.
6. **CV Landmines:** Identifies high-risk resume gaps and provides blunt, bulletproof defenses.

## The Stakeholder-Specific Query Toolkit

### Type 1: The Recruiter (The Gatekeeper)
*Goal: Clear compliance hurdles, show marketability, and defuse immediate profile risks.*
> **Query:** "Give me my 'Opening Hook' and 2 'CV Landmine' defenses for this Recruiter screen."  
> **Query:** "Run 'Why did you leave?' for the Recruiter lens. Keep it under 140 words."

### Type 2: The Hiring Manager (The Decision Maker)
*Goal: Prove day-to-day technical value, address immediate debt, and interrogate operational red flags.*
> **Query:** "Give me the 'Tell me about yourself' response tailored for the Hiring Manager's day-to-day pain."  
> **Query:** "What are my Power Shift Questions to probe the burnout signals in this posting?"

### Type 3: The Skip-Level / Executive (The Strategist)
*Goal: Align with long-term strategy, process efficiency, high-level vision, and cross-functional leadership.*
> **Query:** "Give me my 3 'Power Shift' questions to test their long-term security strategy and team retention."  
> **Query:** "Run 'Why should we hire you?' tailored for the Skip-Level lens, focusing on strategic ROI."

## The "Red Flag Interrogation" Protocol
When evaluating a posting or during the interview, the engine decodes underlying risks into direct questions and translates evasive responses:

| Posting Signal | What to Ask | Red Flag Response Signal | Translation / Internal Reality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Fast-paced environment / shifting priorities"** | "How does the team re-evaluate capacity when emergency requests hit planned projects?" | "You just have to be agile and find a way to get it all done." | Poor project management; scope expands while deadlines stay fixed. |
| **"Thrives under pressure / 24-7 mindset"** | "How is on-call structured, and what is the weekly average for off-hours alerts?" | "We're all-hands-on-deck, so everyone jumps in when big stuff hits... which is often." | Zero boundaries; expectation of constant unpaid overtime and high burnout. |
| **"Wears many hats"** | "Which department owns primary budget and sign-off for this role's core deliverables?" | "It depends on the project—you'll be working with everyone across the board." | Responsibility without authority; multi-department finger-pointing. |

## The "Vocabulary Firewall" Protocol (Soften Rule)
The engine monitors and filters weak, overused text, replacing it with direct language:
· **Hardworking** → Driven / Committed  
· **Team player** → Collaborative  
· **Problem solver** → Analytical / Solution-oriented  
· **Helped** → Facilitated / Enabled  
· **Worked** → Executed / Delivered  
· **Quick learner** → Agile / Adaptive  
· **Self-starter** → Proactive / Initiative-driven  
· **Used** → Leveraged / Applied

## Data Confidence Guardrail
This tool uses an aggressive **Inference Mode** combined with validity checking:
* If critical context is missing, the engine continues using **[Inference]** to make educated assessments grounded in real-world operational realities.
* Every achievement claim links to a valid KPI (including 2026 security-specific metrics like MTTD/MTTR or Attack Surface Reduction). If a metric cannot be inferred logically, it is explicitly flagged as `[Weak Evidence - KPI Assumed]`.
